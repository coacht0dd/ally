Walk-in coolers questions

Before you call

How cold does a walk-in cooler have to be in Georgia?

Georgia requires cold holding at 41°F or below. Food held warmer than that is a priority violation at inspection. That is why a cooler drifting a few degrees is worth a call today rather than next week.

Why is my walk-in cooler icing up?

Icing on the evaporator usually points to a defrost problem, a door or gasket letting warm humid air in, low refrigerant charge, or restricted airflow across the coil. The ice is a symptom — the repair depends on which of those is causing it.

There is water on my walk-in floor. Is that serious?

It usually means the condensate drain is blocked or the drain line has frozen, or that ice on the coil is melting off. It is worth fixing quickly — standing water in a cooler is a slip hazard and a health-inspection finding on its own.

Can you come out after hours for a walk-in?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, including nights and weekends. Call rather than fill in a form when product is at risk.
