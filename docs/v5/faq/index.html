Frequently asked

Before you call

Does Ally Air work on commercial refrigeration and home HVAC?

Yes. Ally Air & Refrigeration handles commercial refrigeration — walk-in coolers, freezers, ice machines, reach-ins and prep tables — as well as commercial HVAC and residential heating and cooling. Most companies in the area do one or the other.

Is there someone available after hours?

Yes. The number is answered by a person 24 hours a day, including nights and weekends. If a cooler or freezer is warming up, call rather than send a form.

What areas does Ally Air cover?

Ally Air is based in Dacula and serves approved communities across Gwinnett County and surrounding areas. Coverage for a specific address is confirmed on the call.

Who owns Ally Air & Refrigeration?

Ben Zeglevski owns the company and is on service calls himself. It is small on purpose — the person who diagnoses the problem is usually the person who repairs it.

How much does a service call cost?

We diagnose on site and give you the price before any work starts, because the cost depends on the fault. Anyone quoting a repair price over the phone before seeing the equipment is guessing.

Do you repair equipment or push replacement?

We repair first whenever repair is the honest answer. The most-quoted review Ally has is from a customer whose furnace was repaired during an ice storm rather than replaced.

How quickly can someone get here?

That depends on the equipment, the fault and what service availability looks like at the time. We will tell you honestly on the call rather than publish a response time we cannot always meet.

Do you offer maintenance agreements?

Yes, for commercial refrigeration and HVAC. Scope and pricing depend on how much equipment is in the building — ask when you call.

What should I do while I wait for a technician?

For refrigeration: keep the doors shut, move product to another working box if you have one, and note what the thermometer reads and when. For a home system: switch it off rather than letting it run on a fault, and clear the area around the indoor and outdoor units.

Can I send a photo of the equipment?

Yes, and it genuinely helps. Text a photo of the unit, the model plate and any error code on the controller to the same number you would call.
