What is actually at risk

Running cost

Auxiliary heat running when the heat pump should be carrying the load is the most expensive way to heat a house, and it happens quietly.

Comfort

A system that cannot switch over leaves a house either too cold or too warm with no way to correct it.

The equipment

A unit iced over and unable to defrost is under mechanical stress every cycle.

Heat pumps questions

Before you call

Why is my heat pump icing up outside?

Some frost in cold damp weather is normal — the unit runs a defrost cycle to clear it. Ice that stays, or builds into a solid sheet, means the defrost cycle is not working: a sensor, the defrost board, the reversing valve, or a charge problem.

Why does my auxiliary heat keep running?

Auxiliary heat should only assist in genuinely cold weather or during defrost. Running constantly usually means the heat pump is not delivering its share — a charge or compressor issue — or the thermostat is configured to bring it on too early. It is worth fixing quickly because it is expensive.

My heat pump blows cool air on a heat call. Is it broken?

Heat pump supply air is cooler than furnace air by nature, so it can feel wrong without being wrong. But if the house is not reaching temperature, or the system will not change over at all, that points to a reversing valve or control fault.
