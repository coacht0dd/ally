Repair or replace

How that decision actually gets made

Not by a rule of thumb, and not by whoever is standing in your utility room with a target.

Age and history

How old the system is, and whether this is the first failure or the third on the same component.

Cost against value

What this repair costs measured against what the unit is actually worth today.

Parts availability

Whether the parts are still obtainable, and how long they take to get.

Fit for the house

Whether the system still matches the house — additions and conversions change the load.

Homeowner questions

Before you call

Do you repair air conditioners, or only replace them?

We repair first whenever repair is the honest answer. Our most-quoted Google review is from a customer whose furnace we fixed during an ice storm instead of selling a new unit — that is the default, not the exception.

What does an AC repair cost?

We diagnose on site and give you the price before any work starts, because the cost depends entirely on the fault. Anyone quoting a repair price over the phone before seeing the system is guessing.

Should I repair or replace an old furnace?

It depends on age, the failure history, parts availability and the cost of this repair against the value of the unit. We will walk you through those on site. Repair is the default when repair genuinely makes sense.

Why does my auxiliary heat keep running?

Auxiliary heat should only assist in genuinely cold weather or during defrost. Running constantly usually means the heat pump is not delivering its share — a charge or compressor issue — or the thermostat is configured to bring it on too early. It is worth fixing quickly because it is expensive.

What residential services does Ally offer?

Air conditioning, furnaces and heat pumps — repair, maintenance and replacement. Anything beyond that, ask on the call rather than assuming from a web page.
