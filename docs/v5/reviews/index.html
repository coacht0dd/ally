On review markup

Why you will not see star ratings in Google for this site

Sites can technically mark up their own reviews so stars appear in search results. Google's spam policies treat self-serving review markup as a violation, so this site does not do it. The 5.0 rating is real and it lives on the Google Business Profile, where Google displays it through its own systems.
