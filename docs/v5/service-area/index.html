Coverage questions

Before you call

What areas does Ally Air serve?

Ally Air serves a 30-mile radius from 2641 Old Peachtree Rd NE in Dacula, Georgia. That covers 37 towns across 11 counties — Gwinnett, Barrow, Fulton, Hall, Jackson and more — including Lawrenceville, Buford, Suwanee, Winder, Duluth, Snellville, Gainesville and Alpharetta.

How far will Ally travel for a commercial refrigeration call?

The published radius is 30 miles from the shop. If you are just outside it and have product at risk, call anyway — commercial refrigeration is usually worth the drive and we would rather tell you honestly than turn you away on a technicality.

Are the distances on this page drive times?

No. They are straight-line distances measured from the shop address, so they are consistent and checkable. Drive time is what actually matters on a service call and it depends on the road and the hour, which is why coverage for a specific address is confirmed on the phone.

Do you charge more the further out you go?

Ask on the call. Pricing is quoted after we understand the equipment and the job rather than published on a web page.
