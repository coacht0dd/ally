Using this site

The information here describes services Ally Air & Refrigeration LLC offers. It is not a quote, and it is not a diagnosis of your equipment. Nothing on this site creates a contract until we have spoken and agreed the work.

No guarantees are made here

This site does not promise an arrival time, a same-visit repair, or a particular outcome. What we can do depends on the equipment, the fault and current service availability, and we will tell you that on the call.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number or text us, we may reply by text about your service request — confirming details, asking a question, or letting you know about the visit. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them, or HELP for help. We do not send promotional texts to people who contacted us about a service call, and we do not share mobile numbers with anyone for marketing.

Photographs you send

If you send a photograph of your equipment we use it to help diagnose the problem. We will not publish a photograph of your premises or your equipment without asking you first.
