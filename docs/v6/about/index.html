The name is not decoration. Everyone needs an ally — and that is as true for a restaurant owner watching a walk-in climb at three in the morning as it is for a family with no heat on the first freezing night of the year.

Ally Air & Refrigeration LLC works two trades that most companies keep separate: commercial refrigeration for the kitchens, markets and shops around Gwinnett County, and heating and cooling for the homes in the same neighborhoods.

That combination is unusual here, and it is deliberate. Refrigeration work sets the standard — a walk-in cannot be allowed to fail, and the diagnosis has to be right the first time because there is product on the shelves. Homeowners get the same technician, working to the same standard.

The company is small on purpose. There is no call center, no commission structure pushing a replacement over a repair, and no script.

You get the owner's judgement, not a salesperson's target.

How we work

Call, assess, diagnose, test

01

Call the 24/7 line

A person answers, day or night. For anything with product at risk, call — that is faster than any form.

02

Describe the situation

The equipment, what the thermometer reads, and whether the business can keep operating.

03

Ally assesses

We work out urgency and what service availability looks like, and tell you honestly what to expect.

04

Diagnose, explain, test

The technician finds the fault, explains the recommended work before starting, and tests operation after.

Arrival times and outcomes depend on the equipment, the fault, and current service availability. We will tell you what to expect on the call rather than promise it on a web page.
