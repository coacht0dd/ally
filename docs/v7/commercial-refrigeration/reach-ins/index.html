Why it matters

What is actually at risk

Held product

Reach-ins hold prepped and portioned product — the most labour-intensive inventory in the building.

Food safety

Georgia requires cold holding at 41°F or below. Food held warmer than that is a priority violation at inspection.

Service speed

A cook who cannot trust a reach-in starts making trips to the walk-in, and the line slows down.

Reach-ins questions

Before you call

Why does my reach-in only lose temperature during service?

A cabinet that holds overnight but drifts during service is usually being asked to do more than it can — doors opening constantly, a condenser that cannot shed heat fast enough, or a gasket that only leaks under load. It is a real fault, not just heavy use.

There is condensation inside the glass door. Is that a problem?

It usually means the door heater has failed, the gasket is letting humid air in, or the cabinet is running warmer than it should. Beyond looking bad on a merchandiser, it is a sign the cabinet is not sealing properly.

Do you work on prep tables as well?

Yes — prep tables and sandwich or pizza rails are the same cold-side equipment and we service them on the same visit if you have both.
