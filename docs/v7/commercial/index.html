Risks we work against

What a cold-side failure actually costs

Inventory

The value inside a commercial box is almost always larger than the cost of keeping it running.

Food safety

Georgia requires cold holding at 41°F or below. Food held warmer than that is a priority violation at inspection.

Operating hours

Cold-side failures close stations and shorten menus long before they close a business.

Energy

Refrigeration that is fighting a fault runs longer and costs more every hour it stays unresolved.

Commercial questions

Before you call

What commercial refrigeration equipment does Ally service?

Walk-in coolers and freezers, ice machines, reach-in refrigerators and freezers, prep tables and display cases — plus the commercial HVAC in the same building.

Do you take after-hours commercial calls?

Yes. The number is answered by a person 24 hours a day, including nights and weekends. Commercial calls with product at risk are triaged ahead of routine work.

Can one company handle both our refrigeration and our rooftop HVAC?

Yes, and that is the point of calling Ally. Most companies in the area do one or the other, which means a restaurant ends up with two contractors and two numbers for one building.

What should I do while I wait for a technician?

Keep doors closed, move product to another working box if you have one, write down what the thermometer is reading and when, and photograph the controller if it is showing a code. We will tell you anything else specific to your equipment on the call.
