Privacy policy.

How Ally Air & Refrigeration LLC handles the information you send through this website or give us on the phone.

Call (404) 941-4599 Request Service

Draft. This text is a starting point written for the build, not legal advice. It must be reviewed by a lawyer and matched to how Ally actually stores and uses data before the site goes live.

What we collect

If you fill in a form on this site we collect the details you type: your name, phone number, email address, service address or ZIP, the equipment involved, and anything you write in the message field. If you attach a photograph we receive that too.

We also record which page the form was sent from, which page you arrived on, where you came from, and any campaign tags in the link you followed. That tells us which parts of this site are useful and which are not.

What we do with it

We use it to respond to your request and to carry out the work. We do not sell it. We do not send marketing messages to people who contacted us about a service call.

Who else sees it

Whatever systems Ally uses to handle scheduling, invoicing and communication will hold this data. Those systems are being finalised and will be named here before launch.

How long we keep it

Records tied to work performed are kept as long as the business is required to keep them. Enquiries that do not lead to work are kept only as long as they are useful.

Your choices

Ask us to correct or delete what we hold about you by calling (404) 941-4599.

This site

This site sets no advertising cookies and embeds no third-party tracking widgets. Analytics, once configured, will be listed here.
